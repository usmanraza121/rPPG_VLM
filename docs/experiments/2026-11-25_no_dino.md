## Learning behavior and interpretation

At epoch37, train waveform correlation=.2492 and validation=.2885. Validation prediction std=.07016 versus target=.92913; rawMSE=.98680 versus zero-outputMSE=1.00200, only1.52%lower. Training MSE also remains near its zero baseline. These show weak raw waveform recovery, not merely a late validation-loss divergence. Low output amplitude alone is not the explanation for FFT HR errors: multiplying a nonzero waveform by a scalar does not move its spectral peak. Do not fix this by post-hoc z-scoring or amplitude amplification.

Validation gate entropy2.1457 versus maximum ln9=2.1972 is near uniform but cannot identify extraction versus weighting failure. The four-block TCN's convolutional receptive field is61frames (~2.03s), within160frameclips (~5.33s). Clip centering also uses the whole clip, so do not equate convolutional support with the entire model's temporal dependency. Longer context is a hypothesis, not an established remedy.

The scheduler reaches its configured1e-4 peak and decays; there is no sign of the previous oversized max_lr problem in this run. Loss and validation HR improve early then largely plateau. Best validation clipHRMAE is12.384 at epoch26 versus12.473 at selected37; these clip diagnostics differ from full-record metrics. Do not choose a new checkpoint using PURE results or infer that extending epochs will solve the HR-range failure.
